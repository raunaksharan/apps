# Sayso Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 27, 2026

Sayso does not collect, store on our servers, or share your personal data.

**Your progress.** Your path, streak, scores and collection are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Accounts and analytics.** Sayso has no accounts and no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. The app does not connect to any server of ours.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details. The app only learns, on your device, whether a subscription is active.

**Reminders.** If you turn on a daily reminder, it is a notification scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

**Sharing.** When you share a result card, you choose where it goes using the iOS share sheet. The card contains only your nickname and score.

**Children.** Sayso is made for teens and adults, is not directed to children under 13, and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes and contact.** If this policy changes we will update this page. Contact: team@notiondemand.com.
