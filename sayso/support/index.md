# Sayso Support
Email: team@notiondemand.com

**Restore a purchase:** open Sayso, tap Settings, then Restore purchases.
**Cancel a subscription:** Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Sayso.
**Where is my progress?** Only on your iPhone, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes it.
**Switch your path:** Settings > Your path.
**Real life or fiction scenes:** Settings > Scenes > Worlds.
**Reminders:** Settings > Notifications on your iPhone, or the reminder option inside Sayso.

Sayso is a game and general guidance, not therapy or professional advice.
