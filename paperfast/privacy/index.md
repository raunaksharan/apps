# Paperfast Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 26, 2026

Paperfast does not collect, store on our servers, or share your personal data.

**Your documents.** Scanning and text recognition happen entirely on your iPhone. Scans are saved only in the app's storage on your device. We never receive them.

**Accounts and analytics.** Paperfast has no accounts and no analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not track you.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details. We receive only whether a subscription is active, from the App Store, on your device.

**On-device usage count.** To offer one free scan, the app keeps a counter of completed scans in your device's Keychain. It never leaves your device.

**Children.** Paperfast is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

**Changes and contact.** If this policy changes we will update this page. Contact: team@notiondemand.com.
