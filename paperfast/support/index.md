# Paperfast Support
Email: team@notiondemand.com

**Restore a purchase:** open Paperfast, tap the gear, then Restore purchases.
**Cancel a subscription:** Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Paperfast.
**Where are my scans?** Only on your iPhone, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes them, so share or save important scans to Files first.
**The camera does not open:** Settings > Paperfast > allow Camera.
